# Content sources

Every rule the mini-site teaches, and every quiz answer, comes from a public page by MOM, ScamShield, the Singapore Police Force (SPF) or GovTech. All pages were read on **28 Oct 2026**. Quotes are copied from the page; where the site paraphrases, the paraphrase is marked.

Mock messages, names, numbers, prices and web addresses in the stories are fictional and labelled EXAMPLE on screen, except the two fake MOM addresses (`mom-sg.org`, `wponlinemomgov.sg.com`), which MOM itself publishes as examples of fake sites.

## Quiz answer key

Total 9 points. Every right answer rests on an official rule. Items that look suspicious but have no official rule behind them are deliberately **not** counted as red flags.

| Item | Right answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1. SMS: sender "MOM-ALERT" | red flag | M1: government SMSes are "sent from the gov.sg SMS Sender ID". S1: from 1 July 2024 "all SMSes from government agencies will use a single gov.sg SMS Sender ID". |
| 1. SMS: `mom-sg.org/pay` | red flag | M1 lists "http://www.mom-sg.org" as a fake; fakes "do not end with \".mom.gov.sg\"". |
| 1. SMS: "reply with your bank login" | red flag | S7: "Government agencies will also not ask for your personal particulars or banking credentials over the phone, via SMSes, or emails." |
| 1. SMS: "Dear worker" and the $350 fine | **not** red flags (on purpose) | No official rule says a greeting or a fine proves a scam. |
| 2. Employer asks you to buy rice | safe | Nothing asked: no money, codes or links. |
| 2. "Bank account is locked. Reply with the OTP" | scam | S3 (OTP requests are a scam sign); S5 (never disclose OTPs). |
| 2. "Lend me your bank card. I will pay you $300" | scam | S6; P6. |
| 2. Friend's Deepavali greeting | safe | Nothing asked. |
| 3. "Cousin" with a new number asks for your code | "No. I never share codes. I will call your old number." | S4: scammers "claim that they had lost their mobile phone and changed phone number"; victims found out when they "contacted their actual acquaintance". S5: never disclose OTPs. |
| 4. Where to get a shopping app | the official app store | S5; P2; M1 (never install apps from unofficial app stores). |
| 5. Chat: "Pay me $500 to keep your job" | red flag | M3: "It is illegal for employers or anyone to demand money from their foreign employees in exchange for keeping their jobs." M5 lists kickbacks as an employment scam. |
| 5. Chat: `wponlinemomgov.sg.com` | red flag | M1 lists "http://wponlinemomgov.sg.com" as a fake. It ends with ".sg.com", not ".mom.gov.sg". |
| 5. Chat: "send me your Singpass password" | red flag | G1: "never disclose your Singpass ID, password, and 2FA details to others." P3: do not disclose Singpass credentials "to anyone, including recruitment agencies". P4. |
| 5. Chat: "Your employer wants to cancel your work permit" | **not** a red flag (on purpose) | The topic alone does not prove a scam. |
| 6. "Let me use your bank account and Singpass" | "No. This is a crime. I will report it." | S6; P6; S7 (TELL the authorities). |
