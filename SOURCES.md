# Content sources

Every rule the mini-site teaches, and every quiz answer, comes from a public page by MOM, ScamShield, the Singapore Police Force (SPF), GovTech or ICA. Two NGO pages (HOME, TWC2) and one news report add real cases as background. Pages were read on **28 Oct 2026**; M7, M11, P7, ICA1, T1 and N1 and the video list on **29 Oct 2026**. Quotes are copied from the page; where the site paraphrases, the paraphrase is marked.

Mock messages, names, numbers, prices and web addresses in the stories are fictional and labelled EXAMPLE on screen, except the three fake MOM addresses (`mom-sg.org`, `wponlinemomgov.sg.com`, `www.mom.gov.sg.official.com.net`), which MOM itself publishes as examples of fake sites (M1). Fake SMS senders are shown as "Likely-SCAM", the label a Singapore phone shows for an unregistered alphanumeric Sender ID (I1).

**Background only (H1, T1, N1):** these add real cases for the team's understanding. H1 and T1 are advocacy pieces that criticise MOM (T1 urges MOM to stop issuing PDF IPA letters; H1 questions how MOM and the Police handled a case), and N1 names an alleged scammer. Do not cite or show them on MOM-branded public screens, and keep the alleged scammer unnamed. Every line on screen rests on an official source.

## Quiz answer key

| Item | Right answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
